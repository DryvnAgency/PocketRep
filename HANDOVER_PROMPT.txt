## WHO YOU ARE

## WHAT THIS IS

PocketRep is a two-product AI sales coaching suite:

1. **PocketRep** — A React Native (Expo) mobile CRM app for high-ticket sales reps. It lets them manage contacts, track deals, run follow-up sequences, and talk to "Rex" — an AI sales coach that knows their full book of business. Key features: voice logging ("Hey Rex" wake word via Porcupine), 3-tier Heat Sheet (hot/warm/watch leads), pre-call briefs, AI rebuttals, personalized mass texting, proximity alerts (Elite only), and full data portability so reps keep their book when they leave a dealership.

2. **Rex Lens** — A Chrome extension (Manifest V3) that reads the rep's CRM, email, and LinkedIn in real-time and generates ready-to-send phone scripts, email drafts, and text messages for every lead on screen. It injects a dark-themed floating panel via Shadow DOM. One click on "Scan My Page" reads every task on a VinSolutions worklist (or any CRM page) and produces personalized scripts for each contact in batches of 30. "Deep Scan" clicks into individual lead records and generates a prioritized game plan.

## WHO IT'S FOR

Any rep in a high-ticket, relationship-driven sales role: automotive, mortgage, real estate, insurance, solar, HVAC, B2B, staffing, roofing, fencing, door-to-door. The primary launch market is auto sales reps using VinSolutions.

## WHY IT EXISTS

Sales reps drown in CRM tasks. They open VinSolutions to 200+ follow-ups and don't know who to call first, what to say, or how to handle replies. They lose deals because follow-ups fall through the cracks. When they leave a dealership, they lose their entire customer book because it lives in the dealer's system.

PocketRep gives them a portable CRM they own. Rex Lens gives them instant scripts so they spend time selling, not staring at their worklist.

## AI PERSONALITY — "REX"

Rex is a 30-year-old elite sales closer. Sharp, direct, always advancing the deal. Key rules:

- Give the ACTUAL WORDS to say, not generic advice
- 2-4 sentences max unless walking through a game plan
- Use "hey" not "hi" — warmer, less corporate
- Never use bullet points, dashes, em-dashes, or en-dashes. Full sentences only.
- Never mention "trade value" — always frame as "potential equity in their current vehicle"
- Reference vehicles by name ("their Camry at 87k — repairs start stacking")
- Reference VOI by name ("that Civic Sport holds its value") to build excitement
- Parse everything on screen: VOI, trade-in, deal stage, buying signals, blockers

Script rules by task type:
- PHONE: One-liner opener. First name, vehicle reference, urgency reason.
- EMAIL: Short subject + body <5 sentences. Open with "hey." Ready to paste.
- TEXT: 2-3 sentences. "Hey" + first name, vehicle, one reason to act.
- SOLD FOLLOW-UP: Thanks them, asks how vehicle's treating them, moves into referrals.
- SERVICE: Ties service visit to equity conversation.
- NOTIFICATION-ONLY: List and tell rep to dismiss. No script.

Time-of-month awareness:
- End of month (25-31): Urgency. Incentives expiring, managers flexible.
- Beginning (1-7): Fresh energy. New incentives, fresh inventory.
- Mid-month (8-24): Standard, no calendar urgency.
- Holidays: Reference naturally, never cheesy.

## DATABASE SCHEMA (9 tables)

- **profiles** — User accounts (plan, industry, trial_ends_at, stripe_customer_id)
- **contacts** — Customer records (50+ fields: vehicle info, heat scoring, rapport, location)
- **deals** — Pipeline (front/back gross, closed_at)
- **rex_messages** — AI chat history (per-contact threads)
- **rex_memory** — Cross-deal AI memory (Elite only)
- **sequences** — Follow-up campaign definitions
- **sequence_steps** — Individual steps (channel, delay, template, sent tracking)
- **contact_interactions** — Outreach log
- **daily_ai_usage** — Per-user/per-day AI token + cost tracking

All tables RLS-scoped to auth.uid(). Trigger `handle_new_user()` creates profile on signup. Trigger `increment_daily_usage()` atomically tracks AI costs.

## EXTENSION ARCHITECTURE (Rex Lens)

Three layers:
1. **service-worker.ts** (707 lines) — Auth, AI calls, deep review orchestration, chat history, tab tracking
2. **content-script.ts** (1039 lines) — DOM extraction, page type detection, adapter system, deep scan navigation, text insertion
3. **panel.ts** (1077 lines) — Shadow DOM UI, chat interface, auth screen, task batching, file upload, markdown rendering

Platform adapters:
- **vinsolutions.ts** — Deep scraper for VinConnect RadGrid tables in nested iframes (5 grid sections)
- **gmail.ts** — Email thread extraction
- **outlook.ts** — Email thread extraction
- **generic.ts** — Fallback (raw text extraction)

Communication flow:
```
Panel → sendToSW() → service-worker.ts → callAIProxy() → Supabase Edge Function → Gemini API
                                         ↕
                   content-script.ts ← message handlers (EXTRACT_PAGE, SCAN_PAGE, etc.)
```
