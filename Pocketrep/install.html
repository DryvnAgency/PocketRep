PocketRep works as a web app right now. Add it to your phone's Home Screen and use it like a normal app — one tap and you're in your Heat Sheet.

iPhone / iPad

Use Safari. Add PocketRep directly to your Home Screen.

1
Open PocketRep in SafariGo to app.pocketrep.pro and sign in.
2
Tap Share ↑Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
3
Tap “Add to Home Screen”Scroll if needed, then choose Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap AddPocketRep now has its own icon. Open it from there.
🤖

Android

Use Chrome. Install PocketRep directly from the browser.

1
Open PocketRep in ChromeGo to app.pocketrep.pro and sign in.
2
Tap the three dots ⋮Open Chrome's menu in the upper-right corner.
3
Tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”The wording can vary by phone.
4
Tap Install / AddPocketRep will appear with your other apps.

💻 Computer

Open app.pocketrep.pro in Chrome or Edge. If you see an Install icon in the address bar, click it. Otherwise use the browser menu and choose Install page as app when available.
