12 car sales follow-up text templates that sound human.

A template should save time without erasing the reason you know this customer. Use these as starting points, replace every bracket with real context, and send only when you have the right to contact the person.

Updated September 1, 202610 minute readCopy and personalize
The formula

Identity + real context + reason now + one easy question. The customer should know who you are, why this message belongs to them, and what the simplest next step is.

“Just checking in” is easy to write and easy to ignore. Strong automotive follow-up earns attention by reconnecting to a specific vehicle, concern, life moment, promise, or ownership milestone.

Before using any template, confirm the details are true. Do not invent urgency, availability, rates, offers, service needs, or customer history.

How to personalize a car sales follow-up text

  1. Identify yourself. Include your name and dealership when the customer may not have you saved.
  2. Use one real detail. Vehicle, color, trade, objection, family need, timing, or previous conversation.
  3. Give the message a reason. A promised follow-up beats a vague “touching base.”
  4. Ask one question. Make it easy to answer from the lock screen.
  5. Match the customer's tone. Sound like the same person they met, not a campaign.

Compliance comes first. Use only customer information you are authorized to use. Respect consent, do-not-contact requests, dealership policy, quiet hours, and applicable messaging laws. A useful template is never permission to spam.

Unsold lead follow-up templates

01 · New inquiry

Fast first response

Use when a customer has asked about a specific vehicle and you are responding through an authorized channel.

Hi [First name], this is [Your name] at [Dealership]. I saw your question about the [Year/Model]. What would help most right now—availability, numbers, or a quick walkaround?

02 · No response

Useful second touch

Add something that moves the conversation instead of repeating the first message.

[First name], it’s [Your name] at [Dealership]. I pulled the details on the [Model] you asked about. Are you mainly comparing price, features, or timing?

03 · Showroom visit

After the customer leaves

Reference the exact thing they liked or the concern that stopped the deal.

Thanks again for coming in, [First name]. You seemed to like the [specific feature] on the [Model], but [concern] was still the question. Want me to work on that piece before we talk again?

04 · Thinking it over

Low-pressure next step

Respect the pause and make the next conversation specific.

Totally understand wanting to think it over, [First name]. When I follow up [agreed day], should I focus on the payment, the trade, or whether the [Model] still feels like the right fit?

05 · Trade

Trade appraisal follow-up

Use only when the customer has discussed a trade with you.

Hi [First name], it’s [Your name]. I’m following up on your [Trade vehicle]. If the trade value lands where you need it, is the [Model] still the one you’d want to move forward with?

06 · Appointment

Confirm without sounding robotic

Restate the time and make it easy to report a change.

Looking forward to seeing you [Day] at [Time], [First name]. I’ll have the [Model] ready to look at. If your timing changes, just text me here.

Sold customer follow-up templates

07 · Delivery

Post-delivery thank-you

Keep the first follow-up about the ownership experience, not the next sale.

[First name], thank you again for trusting me with your [Vehicle]. How did the first drive home go—and is there anything you want me to walk through again?

08 · 30-day

Early ownership check-in

Reference a feature or reason they chose the vehicle.

Hey [First name], you’ve had the [Vehicle] for about a month now. How is [feature/use case they cared about] working out for you?

09 · Service

Service moment

Use only when you have a legitimate service-related reason to reach out.

Hi [First name], it’s [Your name] from [Dealership]. I wanted to make sure you know who to reach if you need help with service for the [Vehicle]. Is everything going smoothly so far?

10 · Referral

Referral ask

Ask after a positive moment, and keep the request human.

I’m glad the [Vehicle] has been treating you well, [First name]. If someone in your circle starts looking, I’d appreciate you sending them my way. I’ll take good care of them.

11 · Ownership timing

Lease or finance milestone

Only reference timing you actually know from authorized records.

[First name], it’s [Your name]. We’re getting closer to the timing we discussed for your [Vehicle]. Are you thinking keep it, trade it, or just explore the numbers before deciding?

12 · Reactivation

Reconnect with an older customer

Lead with the existing relationship, not a fake emergency.

Hey [First name], it’s [Your name]—I helped you with the [Vehicle]. I was reviewing my customer book and wanted to check in. How has it been treating you?

A simple follow-up plan around the templates

A good message still needs a system. After each touch, record what happened and set the next move based on the actual outcome.

Before

Read the context

Know the customer, the vehicle, the last outcome, and why today makes sense.

During

Send one clear message

Keep the draft focused on one reason and one easy response.

After

Record the result

Reply, no answer, appointment, not interested, sold, or another honest outcome.

Five template mistakes to avoid

  • Leaving a bracket or placeholder in the message.
  • Adding fake urgency such as “last chance” when it is not true.
  • Asking three questions in one text.
  • Sending the same vague message to the entire book.
  • Continuing after an opt-out or do-not-contact request.

Car sales text template FAQ

What should a car sales follow-up text say?

Identify yourself, reference the customer's real context, give a clear reason for reaching out, and end with one easy question or next step.

How long should the message be?

Keep it as short as the situation allows. One reason and one question are usually enough. Add detail only when it makes the message more relevant.
