The Rex Workflow

How you close more — starting today.

Four steps. In your truck, at your desk, or on the lot. No manual entry. No forgotten names. No missed intel.

01
Zero-Entry Logging
Throw away the legal pad.

After every meeting, tap the mic and talk. Rex's AI listens, extracts the customer's name, the trade-in value, and the budget timeline — and files it into your book perfectly. No typing. No forgetting. No losing a deal to a sticky note.

  • Works in your truck, in the parking lot, walking to your car
  • Captures names, objections, vehicle specs, and follow-up timing
  • Syncs instantly — pull it up before the next call
⚡ Elite: Rex remembers context across all your deals to spot patterns you missed
02
Predictive Commissions
Wake up to a roadmap, not a to-do list.

Every morning your 3-Tier Heat Sheet shows exactly 5 people who are statistically peaking right now — based on lease windows, mileage patterns, and timing signals. Red. Orange. Gold. You know exactly who to call before you finish your coffee.

  • Surfaces customers due for a trade-up before they start shopping elsewhere
  • Flags lease customers hitting mileage or term limits this month
  • One deal from the Heat Sheet covers your Pro sub for over two years
⚡ Elite: Cross-deal memory — Rex spots patterns across your book that a solo glance would miss
03
The Pre-Call Strike
Walk in like you own the room.

60 seconds before your follow-up, Rex sends a Pre-Call Brief. One tap shows you the rapport notes, the last objection, and the Closer's Angle. One more tap dials. You already know everything before they pick up.

  • Rex reads your notes and builds the brief — you don't write a word
  • Shows what stalled last time and the exact angle to open with
  • One tap to dial — the brief pops up on the screen while the phone rings
⚡ Elite: Cross-deal memory — Rex learns your patterns across every conversation and briefs you sharper over time
04
Rex Lens
Your CRM queue, read for you.

Rex Lens is a Chrome extension that reads your web CRM in real-time. It scans your worklist, ranks every contact by priority, and drafts the next message, email, or call script for each one. Deep Scan clicks into up to 30 contacts at once and builds a ready-to-send game plan for your morning.

  • Reads any web-based CRM worklist and ranks who to call first
  • Drafts texts, emails, and call scripts you can paste in one click
  • Deep Scan: 30 contacts reviewed and triaged in under 2 minutes
