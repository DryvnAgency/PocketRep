Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

You can stop future renewal at any time. The fastest way is the Billing option inside PocketRep, which opens Stripe's secure customer portal.

Cancel before the first trial charge and you will not be billed for a paid subscription.

1. Free Trial

New individual subscriptions include a 7-day free trial when shown at checkout. If you cancel before the first subscription charge, no paid subscription fee is charged. Access may remain available through the end of the trial.

2. Paid Subscription Cancellation

  • You may cancel future renewal at any time.
  • After cancellation, paid access remains available through the end of the current paid billing period unless Stripe shows otherwise.
  • You will not be charged for the next billing cycle after cancellation takes effect.

3. How to Cancel

  • In PocketRep: Open Profile / Settings and choose Billing to open the Stripe Customer Portal.
  • Support: If you cannot access Billing, email service@pocketrep.pro from the email on your account.

4. Refund Policy

Subscription charges are non-refundable except where a refund is required by applicable law. We do not provide partial or prorated refunds for unused time in a billing period. Cancelling stops future renewal; it does not reverse a charge that has already been collected.

5. Account Data

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically mean that all account data is immediately deleted. To request deletion of personal data, email privacy@pocketrep.pro. Deletion requests are handled subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and backup-retention requirements.

6. Resubscribing

If you return after cancelling, the price available at the time you resubscribe may apply. Availability of prior account data depends on whether the account or data was deleted.

7. Contact

Questions about cancellation? Email service@pocketrep.pro.
