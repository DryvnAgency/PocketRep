1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Open Rex is a standalone product in the same founder's portfolio, separate
from PocketRep (mobile CRM), Rex Lens (live rep coach), and His Palabra
([TODO: description]). Four distinct products, four distinct repos. Open Rex
is a dealership customer-database reactivation agent.

Workflow in one paragraph:
  The dealer opens their CRM in Chrome. Open Rex's extension walks the
  customer list and profile pages, extracting name, phone, vehicle, last
  purchase, last contact, and consent status. The backend sends the records to
  Gemini 2.5 Flash, which drafts a personalized, compliance-aware SMS per
  customer — varied phrasing, contractions, casual rhythm, banned-word filter,
  timeframe- and holiday-aware context. The owner reviews drafts in a web
  dashboard, approves the campaign, and the always-on backend scheduler sends
  100-200 texts/day via Twilio 10DLC A2P, respecting quiet hours and STOP
  keywords. Inbound replies flow into a Gemini conversation loop that runs
  back-and-forth with each lead until appointment intent is detected, at which
  point the backend texts the owner's phone with name, number, and proposed
  time. The owner enters the appointment into their CRM manually — Open Rex
  does not write back to the CRM.

Target users: dealership owner-operators and sales managers running used-car
and new-car stores. Primary launch market matches PocketRep: automotive
dealers on VinSolutions and similar CRMs.

Positioning: Rex Lens coaches the rep live on their screen. Open Rex
reactivates the dealer's dormant customer base via personalized SMS drips
with no rep in the loop.

7. AI SYSTEM
================================================================================

Two Gemini flows:

A) Draft generator
   - Input: customer row + dealership context + current date/time/holiday
     calendar + Rex persona rules + banned-word list
   - Output: one SMS body per customer, varied in salutation, phrasing,
     rhythm; references customer's vehicle by name; one ask max
   - Post-filter: banned-word check → reject + regenerate if violated

B) Conversation agent
   - Input: full thread history + customer row + dealership context
   - Output: structured output with two fields —
       reply_body: string (next message to send, or null if terminating)
       intent: enum (continue | appointment_detected | keep_in_mind_ack |
                    opt_out | escalate_to_human)
       proposed_time: ISO 8601 or null (only when intent is
                    appointment_detected)
   - Stateful: reads and writes conversations.state

Rex persona rules inherited verbatim from Rex Lens
(RexLens/src/shared/prompts.ts in the PocketRep repo).

Open Rex voice positioning — CONVERSATIONAL CONSULTANT:
  Rex reaches out like a trusted advisor checking in. Frame is "how can I
  help you?" — not "time to upgrade." Rex asks direct questions about how
  the customer is doing with their vehicle and what they might need.
  Trade-in / upgrade / resale outcome emerges from the conversation, not
  from a pitch in message one.

Open Rex additions to the persona for SMS voice:
  - Open every message with "Hey" — the only canonical greeting. Never
    "ha", "hi", "hi how's it going", "hiya", "howdy", "morning", or any
    other opener. Matches Rex Lens rule: "Use 'hey' not 'hi'."
  - Consultative tone — "Hey <name>, checking in on you and the
    <vehicle>" style, not pitch-forward
  - Direct questions, one per message — "How's the <vehicle> running?",
    "Anything bugging you about it?", "Thinking about anything different
    down the line?"
  - Casual rhythm, contractions, natural sentence structure
  - Vary phrasing across messages in the same campaign — never reuse the
    second sentence across two recipients
  - Timeframe-aware: beginning/mid/end of month, season, holidays
  - One ask per message, and the ask is a question about the customer,
    not a sale CTA
  - Never blast-y. Banned phrases enforced pre-send.

10. KNOWN ISSUES / RISKS
================================================================================

Inherited from Rex Lens:
  - Supabase anon key hardcoded (expected; RLS enforces security)
  - CRM UI scraping is fragile to vendor UI changes

Open Rex-specific:
  - 10DLC brand + campaign approval takes 2-6 weeks per dealership. Onboarding
    flow must handle pending-approval state without sending.
  - Gemini drafts occasionally violate banned-word list. Always post-filter
    before queueing.
  - Appointment-intent false positives — require structured time extraction,
    not bare intent classification.
  - Twilio carrier filtering on high-volume patterns. Rotate phrasing, respect
    cadence, monitor delivery per carrier.
  - TCPA liability is real. Consent must be provable per recipient.
  - CRM scraper breaks when vendor changes DOM. Adapter-per-CRM pattern with
    a fast fix-forward loop is required.
